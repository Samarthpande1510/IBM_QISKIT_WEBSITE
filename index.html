About the event

For three days in October, a lecture hall in Mumbai becomes a quantum lab.

Qiskit Fall Fest is IBM Quantum's global season of student-run events, hosted each year by universities around the world. MPSTME is one of this year's hosts.

Our edition is written for two people at the same time. The first has never opened Qiskit and is not sure quantum computing is for them. The second already has a circuit they want to push further. The keynotes and beginner tracks bring everybody to the same footing, while the hackathon runs across all three days.

You do not need any prior quantum coursework to take part. Bring a laptop and your curiosity, and we will supply the rest, including access to real IBM Quantum hardware over the cloud.

Who this is for

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students across computer science, information technology, electronics, physics and mathematics.
  • Students from any college in India are welcome to register and attend.
  • Hackathon teams are two to four people. If you register alone, we will help you find a team when the hackathon opens.
  • The hackathon is hybrid, so you can take part on campus at MPSTME or remotely from anywhere in India.
  • Every participant receives a certificate, and the top three teams in each bracket receive prizes.
