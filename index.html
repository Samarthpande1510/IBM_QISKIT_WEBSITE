About the event

For three days in October, a lecture hall in Mumbai becomes a quantum lab.

Qiskit Fall Fest is IBM Quantum's global season of student-run events, hosted each year by universities around the world. MPSTME is one of this year's hosts.

Our edition is written for two people at the same time. The first has never opened Qiskit and is not sure quantum computing is for them. The second already has a circuit they want to push further. The keynotes and beginner tracks bring everybody to the same footing, while the hackathon runs across all three days.

You do not need any prior quantum coursework to take part. Bring a laptop and your curiosity, and we will supply the rest, including access to real IBM Quantum hardware over the cloud.

Who this is for

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students across computer science, information technology, electronics, physics and mathematics.
  • Students from any college in India are welcome to register and attend.
  • Hackathon teams are two to four people. If you register alone, we will help you find a team when the hackathon opens.
  • The hackathon is hybrid, so you can take part on campus at MPSTME or remotely from anywhere in India.
  • Every participant receives a certificate, and the top three teams in each bracket receive prizes.

Questions

Everything people keep asking us.

Do I need any background in quantum computing?

No. The beginner tracks start from nothing and are taught by Qiskit Advocates. If you can write a little Python you will keep up comfortably, and if you cannot, the sessions still make sense.

Who is allowed to register?

Students from any college in India. You do not need to be at MPSTME or NMIMS, and there is no restriction on your branch or year.

Does it cost anything?

No. Attendance is free for everyone. Registration is required so we can plan seating and hardware access.

Can I take part without coming to campus?

Yes, for the hackathon, which runs in hybrid mode. You can build remotely from anywhere in India. The keynote addresses and beginner tracks on 24 October are held on campus.

Do I need a team before I register?

No. Hackathon teams are two to four people, and if you register on your own we will help you find a team when the hackathon opens.

What should I bring?

A laptop with Python and Qiskit installed, and an IBM Quantum account set up beforehand. We run a setup desk before the hackathon briefing if anything is not working.

When does registration close?

21 October 2026. We would encourage you to register earlier, since places for the on-campus sessions are limited by the size of the hall.
